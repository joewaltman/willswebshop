## Visual direction

Coast Hwy 101 traditional shop. Deep forest green + oxblood + cream + brass. DM Serif Display wordmark, italic numeral "603" as the typographic mark in the hero.

Distinctive design moves on this build:
1. Address "603" replaces the default star/badge in the hero mark.
2. Monogram circle (initials, brass on green) instead of staff photos.
3. Two-up barbers grid (since only two named staff).
4. Italic numeral with extreme negative tracking for the hero mark.
