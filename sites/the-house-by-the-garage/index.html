About the salon

A little salon
with a lot of heart.

Janea opened The House by The Garage to create a space where clients don't just get great hair — they feel genuinely at home. The kind of place where you know your stylist's name, they know yours, and you've been coming back for years.

With Heidi also behind the chair, every appointment is personal, unhurried, and focused entirely on you. No salon conveyor belt here — just two talented stylists who care deeply about their craft and their clients.

Tucked away on Vulcan Ave in Encinitas, this is the salon your friends have been quietly keeping to themselves.
